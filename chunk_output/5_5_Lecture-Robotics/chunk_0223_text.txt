Standard/Naive metrics:
• Comparing two 2D rotations/orientations θ 1 ,θ 2 ∈ SO(2):
a) Euclidean metric between eiθ1 and eiθ2
b) d(θ 1 ,θ 2 ) = min{|θ 1 − θ 2 |,2π − |θ 1 − θ 2 |}
• Comparing two configurations (x,y,θ) 1,2 in R2:
Eucledian metric on (x,y,eiθ)
• Comparing two 3D rotations/orientations r 1 ,r 2 ∈ SO(3):
Represent both orientations as unit-length quaternions r 1 ,r 2 ∈ R4:
d(r ,d ) = min{|r − r |,|r + r |}
