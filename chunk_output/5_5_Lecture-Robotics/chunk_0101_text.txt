Relative position
Position of a point on link i relative to point on link j
dimension d = 3
parameters link indices i,j, point offset v in i and w in j
kin. map φpos (q) = R-1(φpos − φpos)
iv|jw j iv jw
Jacobian Jpos (q) = R-1[Jpos − Jpos − A × (φpos − φpos)]
iv|jw j iv jw j iv jw
Derivation:
For y = Rp the derivative w.r.t. a rotation around axis a is y(cid:48) = Rp(cid:48) + R(cid:48)p = Rp(cid:48) + a × Rp.
