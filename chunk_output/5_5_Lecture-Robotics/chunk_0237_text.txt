Probabilities & Sets
• Sample Space/domain Ω, e.g. Ω = {1,2,3,4,5,6}
• Probability P : A ⊂ Ω (cid:55)→ [0,1]
e.g., P({1}) = 1, P({4}) = 1, P({2,5}) = 1,
6 6 3
• Axioms: ∀A,B ⊆ Ω
– Nonnegativity P(A) ≥ 0
– Additivity P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) if A ∩ B = ∅
– Normalization P(Ω) = 1
• Implications
0 ≤ P(A) ≤ 1
P(∅) = 0
A ⊆ B ⇒ P(A) ≤ P(B)
P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)
P(Ω \ A) = 1 − P(A)
