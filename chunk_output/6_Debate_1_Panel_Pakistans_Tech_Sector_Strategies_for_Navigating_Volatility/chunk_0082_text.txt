What I Would Not Do
I would not advise a Pakistani tech business to:
• Wait for PayPal, Stripe, tax reform, or STZ incentives before fixing its own operating model.
• Depend on one large foreign client as the whole growth plan.
• Use informal remittance channels to solve short-term payment friction.
• Hire aggressively on projected revenue rather than collected cash.
