Position 1: The VC's 'Offshore Everything' Escape Plan
The VC's argument is a pragmatic, if cynical, surrender. It advocates for moving money and corporate
structures out of Pakistan to protect returns. While I understand the logic from a fund manager's
perspective, as a national strategy, it's a disaster. It accelerates brain drain and turns Pakistan into a
low-cost talent farm with no IP, no value capture, and no ecosystem.
