6. Placement-linked digital skills programmes
I will endorse industry-led finishing schools and bootcamps only if they are placement-linked and
co-financed.
A realistic pilot may cover 20,000 trainees at an estimated cost of PKR 120,000-180,000 per trainee,
implying a gross programme cost of PKR 2.4-3.6 billion. If structured as a 50% matching grant, the
public contribution would be approximately PKR 1.2-1.8 billion. This must be subject to Finance Division
