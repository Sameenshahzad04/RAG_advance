• Assumption 5: Special Technology Zones can help if they are treated as productivity and export
platforms, not merely real estate projects. Status: Verified in law, implementation gap remains. The
Special Technology Zones Authority Act, 2021 provides the legal basis for STZs and includes fiscal
incentives such as time-bound exemptions. The effectiveness of individual zones depends on
infrastructure, tenant quality, governance, and export outcomes.
