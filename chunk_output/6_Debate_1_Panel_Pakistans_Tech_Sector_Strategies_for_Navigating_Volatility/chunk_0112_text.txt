Assessment of the Founder & CEO's Proposal
The Founder's "Fortress Pakistan" strategy is a compelling narrative of self-reliance. However, it is an
anecdotal solution from what appears to be a well-capitalized company. It romanticizes a high-cost,
high-risk operational posture that is neither scalable nor advisable for most businesses.
