Red Line
Absence of an SBP circular (or equivalent enforceable mechanism) guaranteeing automatic repatriation
of up to USD 2 million per portfolio company per annum within 30 calendar days without discretionary
FBR or SBP case-by-case approval.
Key Assumptions
• Currency devaluation will continue at 15-25% annually (verified via SBP historical data
2018-2023).
• Export-oriented SaaS and fintech models can achieve 60%+ gross margins (verified via fund's
existing portfolio benchmarks).
