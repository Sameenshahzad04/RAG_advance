3. I will not endorse a government guarantee that
Stripe or PayPal will enter Pakistan within 12 months
I reject any policy red line that requires the Government of Pakistan to guarantee onboarding by a
specific private foreign payment company within 12 months. SBP can regulate, clarify, facilitate, and
process applications; it cannot compel Stripe, PayPal, or any other firm to enter the market.
