2. Zero-Time Policy on Government Schemes
(Concession to Founder & CEO's Round 2 Argument)
• The Tactic: Freeze all direct applications for Special Technology Zones (STZs) or complex
government grants if you are under 50 employees.
• Why I Concede: The Founder's Round 2 argument that navigating STZ bureaucracy consumed
30% of his senior management time for six months is a chilling operational reality. For a growing
firm, this time is better spent on sales.
