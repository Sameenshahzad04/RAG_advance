10. Build a Weekly Management Dashboard
In volatility, monthly review is too slow. Your leadership team should review the following weekly:
• Cash in bank.
• Runway in months.
• Receivables by age.
• Revenue by client.
• Revenue by geography.
• Gross margin by project or product line.
• Payment channel exposure.
• Hiring commitments.
• Delivery delays.
• Client churn or expansion signals.
• Tax/compliance deadlines.
