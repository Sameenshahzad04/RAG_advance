My Red Line: Capital Preservation
My board and I have a fiduciary duty to protect this company from existential threats, and the single
biggest threat is arbitrary and unpredictable foreign exchange controls by the State Bank of Pakistan
(SBP).
Therefore, my red line is not a policy request, but an internal risk management policy: We will not hold
more than 90 days of operational expenditure (OpEx) onshore in PKR or in our Special Foreign Currency
