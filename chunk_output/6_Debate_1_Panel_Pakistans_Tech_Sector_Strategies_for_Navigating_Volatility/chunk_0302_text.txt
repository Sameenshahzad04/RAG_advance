This figure is not verified and is likely implausible for large EU corporate procurement. Many European
enterprises pay SaaS vendors through bank transfer, SEPA, procurement portals, or invoice-based
payment terms rather than Stripe alone. Stripe is highly relevant for self-serve SaaS and SMEs, but the
claim that 90 percent of SaaS vendors are paid through Stripe-style flows requires evidence from the
buyer's accounts payable system or industry benchmarks.
