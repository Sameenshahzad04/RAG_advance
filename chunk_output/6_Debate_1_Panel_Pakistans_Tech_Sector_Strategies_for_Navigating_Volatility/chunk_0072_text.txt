8. Talent Strategy: Retain the Core, Make the
Bench Variable
Pakistani tech firms face salary inflation, remote-work competition, migration, and uneven graduate
quality. The answer is not indiscriminate hiring.
Recommended structure:
• Keep a permanent core team for architecture, client communication, delivery management, and
quality assurance.
• Use contractors for burst capacity, but standardize NDAs, IP assignment, confidentiality, and
security obligations.
