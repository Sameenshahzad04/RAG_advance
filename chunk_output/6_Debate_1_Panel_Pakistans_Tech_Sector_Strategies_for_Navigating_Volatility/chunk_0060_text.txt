3. Change Contract Terms Before You Chase
More Leads
Many Pakistani tech firms accept weak commercial terms because they are eager for foreign revenue. In
volatility, weak terms become a financing burden.
For services companies, I would recommend:
• 30% to 50% upfront payment for new clients. This is an unverified commercial benchmark, not a
legal requirement.
• Milestone billing every 2 weeks or monthly, not only at final delivery.
