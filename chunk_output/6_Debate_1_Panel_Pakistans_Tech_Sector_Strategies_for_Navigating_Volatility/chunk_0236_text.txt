5. Improve Business Continuity, Cybersecurity, and
Data Governance
Volatility is not limited to macroeconomics. Firms face power interruptions, internet disruptions, cyber
incidents, client-side compliance requirements, and reputational risk. A serious exporter must be able to
demonstrate continuity and trust.
Business recommendations:
• Maintain cybersecurity policies, access control, secure backups, incident response procedures,
and client data segregation.
