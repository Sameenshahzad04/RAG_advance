Red Line Status: Unaddressed and Hardened
My red line from Round 1 was simple: an operational framework for Stripe or a full-service PayPal
within 12 months. This has not been addressed. In fact, it's been sidestepped with proposals that treat
the symptoms—repatriation friction and messy documentation—while ignoring the disease. The
disease is that we are commercially un-bankable by default in the eyes of the global digital economy.
