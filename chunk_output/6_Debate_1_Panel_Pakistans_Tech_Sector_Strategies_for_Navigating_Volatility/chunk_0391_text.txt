2. Fiscal approval for tax and STZ incentives
The Ministry of IT cannot unilaterally commit the federation to multi-year tax treatment or STZ tax
expenditure. Finance Division concurrence is mandatory.
Escalation path: Finance Division, FBR, Ministry of IT, STZA, and Law Division should prepare a fiscal
note for ECC/Cabinet consideration before budget finalisation.
