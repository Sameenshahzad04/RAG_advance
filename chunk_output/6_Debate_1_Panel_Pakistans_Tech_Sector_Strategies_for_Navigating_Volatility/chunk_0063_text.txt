4. Move Up the Value Chain: Generic
Outsourcing Is Too Exposed
If your firm sells generic web development, mobile apps, QA, or staff augmentation, you are exposed to
global price compression and AI-enabled competition. This does not mean abandoning services; it
means narrowing your positioning.
