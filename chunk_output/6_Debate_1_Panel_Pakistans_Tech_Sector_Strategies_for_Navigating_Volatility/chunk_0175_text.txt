Justification: The reconciliation certificate is a valuable tool for formalization, but making it a
sequential gatekeeper would delay the highest-impact reform—payment gateway access—by
an unacceptable 18-24 months, as argued by both the Founder (Round 3) and the VC (Round
2). By mandating parallel implementation, we achieve the DG's goal of improved
documentation without sacrificing the industry's immediate need for transactional efficiency.
