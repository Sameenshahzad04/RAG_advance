2. A 10-Year 'Stable Tax' Pact on IT Exports: The annual drama around the national budget—will
the tax exemption stay or go, will the rate be 0.5% or 1%, will new reporting be required—is
paralyzing. We cannot make long-term hiring or R&D plans. We need a legislated, 10-year policy
of a fixed, simple 1% final tax on export remittances. Predictability is more valuable than any
temporary exemption.
