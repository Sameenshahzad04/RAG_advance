I will endorse a predictable tax framework for IT and ITeS exporters, but it must be reviewable and
linked to formal remittance, registration, and compliance.
The preferred model is:
• A three-year rolling framework for registered exporters.
• Grandfathering protection against retrospective interpretation for compliant taxpayers.
• Renewal based on export growth, formalisation, employment, and fiscal performance.
