7. I will not endorse STZ incentives without
tax-expenditure reporting
I reject STZ expansion without transparent reporting. If STZ incentives are not linked to exports, jobs,
investment, and tenant performance, they risk becoming rent-seeking instruments. Finance Division
must quantify the revenue cost before any major expansion.
