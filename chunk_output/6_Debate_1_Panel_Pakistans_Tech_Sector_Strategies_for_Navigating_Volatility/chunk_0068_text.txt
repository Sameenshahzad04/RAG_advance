6. Create a Compliance File Before You Need It
Most founders prepare documentation only when a bank, investor, client, or tax authority asks. That is
too late.
Your monthly compliance file should contain:
• Signed client contracts or purchase orders.
• Invoices with service descriptions matching actual work.
• Proof of delivery or acceptance.
• Bank remittance records.
• Payroll records.
• Contractor agreements.
• Tax filings and acknowledgements.
