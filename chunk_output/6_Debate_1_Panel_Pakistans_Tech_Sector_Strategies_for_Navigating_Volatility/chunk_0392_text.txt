3. SBP's acceptable ceiling for foreign currency
retention and outward payments
My recommended starting ceiling is USD 250,000-500,000 per verified exporter annually, but SBP must
model FX exposure, AML/CFT safeguards, and banking-sector capacity. The VC's proposed USD 2
million ceiling remains unresolved and, in my view, excessive without evidence.
Escalation path: SBP-led working group with Finance Division, Ministry of IT, PSEB, banks, and industry
